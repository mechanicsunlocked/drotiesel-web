Measured, not guessed

Every wheel goes through the dial

Scroll, and the wheel turns in the truing stand. The dial indicator under the rim reads the radial runout in hundredths of a millimetre. The brass marks are at ±0.15 mm.

The values go on the job tag

We measure the spoke tension and the runout before the wheel leaves the stand, and we write the values on the tag. You can see what we did.

Custom wheels

Wheels built for your riding

A hand-built wheel is made for one rider and one bike. This is how we do it.

  1. Talk

    Your bike, your weight, how and where you ride, your budget.

  2. Choose the parts

    We propose rims, hubs and spokes. You decide.

  3. Build

    Laced, tensioned, stress-relieved and trued by hand.

  4. Hand-over

    With a build card: spoke lengths, tension and runout of your wheels.

The name

Why a donkey?

In Luxembourgish a bicycle is a Drotiesel: a wire donkey. A wheel is wire under tension, and a donkey carries its load every day without complaint. That is what we build.
